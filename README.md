# Information Theory and Coding - TEL417

MATLAB projects and reports for the **Information Theory and Coding** course at the Technical University of Crete (Spring 2025).

## Projects

> [Project 1](Project%201) - **Entropy and Typical Sequences.** Information-theoretic puzzles (e.g. the hat-color problem) and the entropy of a geometric random variable. Probabilities of the typical sequences of an i.i.d. binary source, and their link to data compression.

> [Project 2](Project%202) - **Source Coding.** Entropy computation, and Huffman, Shannon and Shannon-Fano-Elias codes. Encoding and decoding are verified, and the average codeword length is compared with the entropy through Monte Carlo simulation.

> [Project 3](Project%203) - **Channel Coding and Capacity.** Simulated bit error rate (BER) against block length for different code rates. Mutual information and the capacity of Binary Symmetric Channels (BSC), including cascaded channels.
